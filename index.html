This is a simulation. The answers were rendered in advance and saved as files, so clicking a question plays a video that already existed — no model runs in your browser and no server is called. It shows you the output and the playback. It shows you nothing about speed: there is no computation here to be fast or slow at, and an earlier version of this page was slower than the real thing because it made you sit through a "let me think" clip that was covering a wait that did not exist. Point the same player at your own LLM endpoint and it runs live. What that costs is measured, not simulated, and it is on the latency page: 11.9 s end to end for one sentence on the card benchmarked here — the number a filler clip is for. 這是模擬。 答覆是事先算好存成檔案的，所以你按下去播的是一支本來就存在的影片——瀏覽器裡沒有模型在跑，也沒有呼叫任何伺服器。它給你看的是產出與播放，關於速度它什麼都沒告訴你：這裡根本沒有運算可言，而這個頁面的前一版還比真實情況更慢，因為它讓你先看完一支「讓我想想」——那支片在蓋一段不存在的等待。把同一個播放器接上你自己的 LLM 端點，它就會即時運作。那條路的代價是量出來的、不是模擬的，寫在延遲那一頁：在這裡量的卡上，一句話端到端 11.9 秒——墊詞片就是為了那個數字存在的。

Three looks, one pivot frame each三套外觀，各自一張樞紐幀

Switching outfit is not a costume change on one video. Each look is its own clip family with its own pivot frame — the single frame every clip in that family begins and ends on — and the families are not interchangeable. Measured between two of them: 50.6 mean and 202 worst-block out of 255, which is simply what two different pictures look like. So looks are switched deliberately, never mid-rotation. 換服裝不是同一支影片換件衣服。每一套外觀是自己的一個片段家族，各有一張樞紐幀——那是該家族每一支片起訖的同一格——而家族之間不能互換。量兩套之間：平均 50.6、最差區塊 202（滿分 255），那就是兩張不同的畫面該有的樣子。所以換外觀一定是使用者主動觸發，絕不在輪替中途發生。

The finding that changed our own conclusions推翻我們自己結論的那個發現

Components do not have a fixed size. They take less when there is less.元件沒有固定大小。可用的少，它就拿得少。

The cloned-voice TTS held 5.1 GB with 24 GB available and ran in 2.7 GB when we constrained the card to 8 GB — same work, 18–26% slower on short sentences and no slower at all on long ones. 克隆聲音 TTS 在有 24 GB 可用時佔 5.1 GB，把卡限制到 8 GB 時跑在 2.7 GB——同樣的工作，短句慢 18–26%，長句完全沒慢。

That breaks the arithmetic every guide does, including our own first draft. We published “8 GB is impossible — the TTS alone is 5.1 GB and only 4.8 GB is free”, then tested it and watched the whole stack run in 7,052–7,615 of 8,192 MiB. The wrong prediction is still in the documentation, because it is the clearest argument we have against sizing a machine from someone else’s numbers — including ours. 這打掉了每一份指南都在做的加總，包括我們自己的初稿。我們發布過「8 GB 不可能——光 TTS 就 5.1 GB，而只剩 4.8 GB」，然後實測，看著整套堆疊跑在 8,192 MiB 之中的 7,052–7,615。那個錯誤預測還留在文件裡，因為它是我們手上最有力的證據，說明不該用別人的數字去推算一台機器——包括我們的數字。

Which card do you need你需要哪張卡

Smaller tiers were tested by holding VRAM until the card had only that much free. That emulates capacity, which decides whether things load and run. It does not emulate a different GPU’s bandwidth — so “it fits” transfers to real hardware and “it is this fast” does not. 較小的等級是用工具佔住顯存、只留下目標容量來測的。那模擬的是容量，決定東西載不載得進去、跑不跑得動。它不模擬另一張 GPU 的頻寬——所以「裝得下」可以套用到真實硬體，「有這麼快」不行。

What that demo is actually doing那個 demo 實際上在做什麼

Asking a question cuts straight to the answer, interrupting whatever idle clip is on screen. An earlier version played a “let me think” filler first, because that is what production does: after the model choice, the pipelining and the caching, what is left is genuine computation, and the remaining move is to cover it with something the character is plausibly doing. Nothing is computed here, so the filler was covering nothing and made every answer four seconds late. A filler is worth exactly as much as the wait it hides. 問一個問題會直接切到答案，就地打斷當下正在播的待機片。先前的版本會先播一支「讓我想想」的墊詞片，因為生產環境就是那樣做的：在選完模型、做完管線化與快取之後，剩下的是真實運算，而剩下的招數就是用一件角色合理會做的事把它蓋起來。但這裡沒有任何東西在算，所以墊詞片什麼都沒蓋到，只讓每個答覆晚了四秒。墊詞片的價值，剛好等於它蓋掉的那段等待。

Every line marked handover is a moment a visual defect can occur — they all happen at a switch. Resting replays the same clip in place, which skips the handover entirely, and the rotation is built around that. 每一行標著 handover 的地方，都是視覺缺陷可能發生的那一刻——它們全部發生在切換時。休息時是原地重播同一支片，完全跳過交接，而輪替就是圍繞這件事設計的。

The ! button queues a clip that does not exist. Two bugs were found by pressing it — a load failure mid-handover froze the player permanently, and the recovery then chose the same broken clip again. Both were in the shipped component this was extracted from, and the unit tests passed throughout. ! 按鈕會排入一支不存在的片段。有兩個 bug 是靠按它發現的——交棒進行中的載入失敗會讓播放器永久凍結，而它的恢復又會再挑同一支壞片段。兩個在被抽取的那個上線元件裡都存在，而單元測試從頭到尾都是綠的。

The clip library is where the architecture gets tested, and it failed five times before it was shippable — and the checker still returns 1 on it. Every clip here — idle and answer alike — is rendered by the same lip-sync engine over a base clip that begins and ends on the pivot frame, with the audio padded to exactly that clip's length — most bases are 124 frames, three are 107. Both halves matter. Padding is what makes a talking clip end on the pivot: the engine renders ceil(seconds × fps) frames of the base from frame 0, so without it the clip stops wherever the speech ran out. Leading silence is what makes it start there — otherwise frame 0 already carries the first phoneme's mouth shape, which measured 5.9 out of 255 in the mouth region while the whole-frame mean said 3.19 and looked fine. And rendering the idle clips through the same engine is what makes the two families match: the engine regenerates the mouth on every frame including silence, and that generated mouth sits 6.22 from the untouched footage, so a library mixing the two steps the jaw at every join between them. 片庫才是這套架構真正被檢驗的地方，而它在能出貨之前失敗了五次——而且檢查器到現在對它仍然回傳 1。 這裡每一支片——待機與答覆——都是同一套對嘴引擎在一支起訖都落在樞紐幀的底片上算出來的， 音訊補到剛好等於那支底片的長度——多數底片是 124 幀，有三支是 107 幀。兩件事缺一不可。補長度決定講話片停在樞紐幀：引擎會從第 0 幀 起算 ceil(秒數 × fps) 幀，不補就停在語音用完的那一格。前置靜音決定它起於 樞紐幀，否則第 0 幀已經帶著第一個音素的嘴型——量在嘴部區域是 5.9（滿分 255），而整幀 平均只有 3.19，看起來完全正常。而把待機片也送進同一套引擎，是為了讓兩個家族對得上： 引擎連靜音時都會重生嘴部，那張生成的嘴離原始素材 6.22，兩者混在同一個庫裡， 每一次跨家族的接點下巴都會跳一下。

Other things that surprised us其他意外的發現

The expensive part is the voice, not the video貴的是聲音不是影像

People budget for the lip-sync model. The cloned-voice TTS cost more than twice as much (5.1 GB vs 2.4 GB), and it is where the user’s waiting time goes: 11.9 s end to end for one sentence, of which lip-sync is 1.75 s. 大家都在預算對嘴模型。克隆聲音 TTS 貴了兩倍多（5.1 GB 對 2.4 GB），而且使用者的等待時間也花在那裡：一句話端到端 11.9 秒，其中對嘴只佔 1.75 秒。

Context costs almost as much as the model上下文幾乎跟模型一樣貴

Going from Ollama’s 4096-token default to a 32k window increases an 8B model’s footprint by 87%. A companion needs a persona and a history, so this is not optional. 從 Ollama 預設的 4096 token 拉到 32k 視窗，8B 模型的佔用增加 87%。伴侶需要人設與對話歷史，所以這筆錢躲不掉。

On Windows your tools lie about capacity在 Windows 上工具會騙你

Per-process VRAM does not exist under WDDM, and ollama ps reported “17 GB, 100% GPU” where the measured delta was 11.5 GB. It never once said it had offloaded anything. Throughput is the only honest fit signal. WDDM 之下沒有 per-process 顯存，而 ollama ps 回報「17 GB，100% GPU」時實測 delta 是 11.5 GB。它一次都沒說過自己分流了。吞吐量是唯一誠實的訊號。

Residency is cheap; concurrency is not駐留便宜，併發才貴

A 27B model sitting resident — even one that filled the card to 98% — cost nothing measurable. The same model actively generating made lip-sync 1.6× slower. 一顆 27B 模型駐留著、即使把卡填到 98%，成本量不出來。同一顆模型真的在生成時，讓對嘴慢了 1.6 倍。

Composition decides how much GPU you need構圖決定你需要多少 GPU

A widely-shared low-VRAM build runs lip-sync at 256×256 and its author says the blur “basically isn’t noticeable” — true, for their composition: side-lit, dark, subject to one side. Front-lit close-ups are the hardest case there is. 一個廣為分享的低顯存建置把對嘴跑在 256×256，作者說那個糊「基本上看不出來」——那是真的，對他的構圖而言：側光、偏暗、角色在一側。正面明亮的大特寫是最難的情況。

3 of 4 lip-sync models forbid commercial use四個對嘴模型有三個禁止商用

Including the one that makes the low-VRAM story work, and including two that are Apache-2.0 on top and ship non-commercial face-detection weights underneath. We audited this after recommending one of them. 包括讓低顯存故事成立的那一個，也包括兩個上層是 Apache-2.0、底下卻挾帶非商業臉部偵測權重的。我們是在推薦了其中一個之後才稽核出來的。

How to know these numbers are real怎麼知道這些數字是真的

You should not take them on trust, and the repository is built so you do not have to. Every figure carries where it came from: 你不該相信它們，而這個 repo 就是為了讓你不必相信而建的。每一個數字都帶著它的來源：

recorded In the committed results file. You can check it.在提交的結果檔裡。你可以查。
session Measured on this machine, but the tool wrote no file. Real, unverifiable by you — and a debt we owe.在這台機器上量的，但工具沒有寫檔。是真的，但你無法查證——而那是一筆我們欠的債。
source From the source project’s engineering log, not re-measured here.出自來源專案的工程紀錄，這裡沒有重新量過。
third-party Someone else’s published number. Not verified.別人發表的數字。未經驗證。

The tools that produced them are in the repository and run against any HTTP service, not just ours. Each one’s header documents a way we measured it wrong first. 產生它們的工具都在 repo 裡，可以打任何 HTTP 服務，不限於我們的。每一支的檔頭都記錄著我們先前是怎麼量錯的。
